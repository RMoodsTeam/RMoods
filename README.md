## Introducion
RMoods is a comprehensive NLP analysis tool for Reddit. It leverages advanced natural language processing techniques to analyze post and comments under each subreddit to see the reaction of user for each topic.

## About
RMoods is designed to provide deep insights into the vast amount of data generated on Reddit. By applying advanced NLP techniques, RMoods can analyze the sentiment, detect sarcasm, identify key topics, and much more. This tool is invaluable for researchers, marketers, and anyone interested in understanding the behavior and trends within Reddit communities.


## Features
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Analyze the sentiment of Reddit posts and comments to understand the overall mood.
- **Language Detection**: Identify the most commonly used language in posts and comments.
- **Sarcasm Detection**: Detect sarcasm in posts and comments.
- **Keyword Extraction**: Extract important keywords from discussions to identify key topics.
- **Spam Detection**: Identify spam in posts and comments.
- **Political Affiliation Detection**: Determine if the text leans towards Republicans or Democrats.
- **Hate Speech Detection**: Detect hate speech in posts and comments.
- **Clickbait Detection**: Identify clickbait titles in posts.
- **AI-Generated Text Detection**: Determine if the text was written by a human or generated by AI.
